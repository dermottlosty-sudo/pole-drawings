Design tables

CE/C36 lines were originally designed to provide a 33kV single circuit arrangement utilising 175mm ACSR conductor.

Basic span : 250 feet (76.2m) Pole Grade Single stout or E/Stout poles with Rutter poles at Section angle points.

MWT’s used in the design were based around 23kN

To enable these lines to be refurbished or diverted, the following design tables have been produced
